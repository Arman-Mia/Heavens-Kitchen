Our Story

A neighbourhood kitchen that feels like home.

Tucked near the 206 Foot Bridge in Samarpally, Kestopur, Heavens Kitchen is where locals come for tasty Indian and Chinese food that doesn’t burn a hole in the pocket.

From garlic-tossed chowmein and pan-fried momos to a wholesome everyday thali, every plate is cooked fresh, served hot and kept spotlessly clean. Stay for the open-air seating and the warm glow of the fireplace — evenings here are made for friends and family.
